Convert On Go was built with one mission: make file conversion fast, free, and 100% private. No uploads, no servers, no compromises.

Our Story

We got tired of uploading sensitive documents to sketchy websites just to convert a PDF to Word. Most "free" converters actually upload your files to their servers — meaning your private contracts, personal photos, and business documents are sitting on someone else's computer.

So we built Convert On Go. A file converter that works entirely in your browser. When you drag a PDF into our tool, it never leaves your device. The conversion happens locally using JavaScript libraries running right on your computer or phone.

How It Works

100% Browser-Side

All processing happens inside your browser using WebAssembly and JavaScript. Zero data transmission.

Instant Results

No upload queues or server processing. Your computer's CPU does the work in seconds.

No Registration Required

Start converting immediately. Register only if you want unlimited conversions — still free.

Works Anywhere

Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, Android — any modern browser works. No installation needed.

What You Can Convert

  • PDF → Word (.docx) — Extract text and structure from PDFs into editable Word documents
  • Word (.docx) → PDF — Convert Word documents to universally readable PDF format
  • JPG → PNG — Convert JPEG images to PNG with transparency support
  • PNG → JPG — Compress PNG files into smaller JPEG format
  • WebP → PNG — Convert modern WebP images to widely compatible PNG

Built on Open Source

We stand on the shoulders of giants. Convert On Go is powered by trusted open-source libraries:

  • pdf-lib — PDF creation and manipulation
  • mammoth.js — Word document to HTML conversion
  • docx.js — Word document creation
  • pdf.js — PDF text extraction (by Mozilla)

Our promise: Your files are yours. We never see them, store them, or share them. Everything happens on your device. That's not a marketing claim — it's how the technology actually works. We cannot access your files even if we wanted to.
