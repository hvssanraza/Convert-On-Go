Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

At Convert On Go, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our file conversion service.

1. No Data Collection

Convert On Go is designed with privacy as a core principle. All file conversions happen entirely within your browser using JavaScript libraries. Your files are never uploaded to any server. We do not have the ability to access, view, or store your documents or images.

2. Information We Collect

Registration Data: When you voluntarily register for unlimited conversions, we collect your name and email address through our Formspree form. This information is:

  • Stored securely by Formspree (our form processing partner)
  • Used only to identify your registration status
  • Never sold, shared, or used for marketing without your consent

Local Storage: Your registration status and name are stored in your browser's localStorage for convenience. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by signing out or clearing your browser data.

3. Usage Data

We track the number of conversions performed by free-tier users (stored locally in your browser). This count is used solely to enforce the 5-conversion limit for non-registered users. No usage data is sent to our servers.

4. Cookies

Convert On Go does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. The only data stored in your browser is your registration status and conversion count via localStorage, which is essential for the service to function.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Formspree: Processes registration form submissions. Formspree Privacy Policy
  • CDN Libraries (Cloudflare, Unpkg): Deliver JavaScript libraries required for file conversion. These services may log standard server access data.
  • Google Fonts: Provides fonts for our interface. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense: May display advertisements. Google Privacy Policy

6. Data Security

Since file processing occurs entirely in your browser, there is no risk of server-side data breaches affecting your documents. Your files are never transmitted to or stored on any external server.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Use the service without providing any personal information
  • Clear your registration data at any time by signing out
  • Request deletion of your Formspree submission data by contacting us

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
